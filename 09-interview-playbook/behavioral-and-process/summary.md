# Behavioral, Process and Preparation: One-Page Summary

- **Process:** resume screen > recruiter call > technical screen > take-home > onsite loop (coding, design, behavioral) > committee/references > offer. Run processes in parallel.
- **Pitch (60-90 s):** present, past (with a number), future (why this company).
- **STAR(-L):** Situation (15%), Task (10%), **Action (50%, "I")**, Result (25%, numbers), + Learning. Build a **story bank of 6-8** covering impact, conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity, hard problem, collaboration, deadlines.
- **Failure questions:** real mistake, own it, fix, **process change**, lesson. Never blame.
- **Coding rounds:** clarify, narrate, small steps, test with your own cases, take hints, summarize complexity. **Take-homes:** README, tests, trade-offs, scope control.
- **Ask good questions:** success in 6 months, on-call/process, technical debt, decisions, growth, remote collaboration.
- **Resume:** one page, ATS-friendly, action + what + how + **measurable result**, tailored keywords. **LinkedIn/GitHub:** headline, pitch, 3-5 pinned repos with READMEs.
- **Negotiate:** let them name the range first, consider total compensation, written offer, polite evidence-based ask, competing offers help, never lie.
- **12-week plan:** complexity/DS > algorithms > OOP/Python > databases > OS/networking > system design > behavioral > mocks. Daily: 1-2 timed problems, concepts, spaced review; redo misses after 1 and 3 weeks.
- **Mocks:** at least 4-6, speak while coding, record, get specific feedback.
